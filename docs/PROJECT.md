# Project Charter — First Paying Plumber

## Objective
Acquire at least one paying UK plumbing/heating client within 30 days for a done-for-you missed-lead recovery and qualification service.

## Customer
Primary ICP:

- UK domestic plumbing/heating business
- Approximately 2–8 engineers
- Limited company preferred for first outreach wave
- Owner still operational / often on the tools
- No full-time receptionist, or clear missed-call risk
- Phone-led inbound demand
- Emergency plumbing, boiler repair or heating work is especially attractive
- Strong Google presence / reviews is a positive signal
- Existing job-management software is acceptable; we sit in front of it

## Customer Problem
A prospective customer calls while the plumber is driving, under a sink, on another job or otherwise unable to answer. The prospect then calls a competitor.

## Product Promise
When the business cannot answer an inbound lead, the customer receives an immediate response, basic qualification and a clear path to the next step instead of being lost.

## Commercial Offer
Founding-client target pricing:

- Setup: £99
- Initial 30-day pilot: £99–£149
- Post-pilot target: £249–£299/month + usage where appropriate

Pricing may be adjusted based on real sales evidence. Do not use “free” by default; the project requires a paying client.

## Core Success Metric

```text
PAYING_CLIENTS >= 1
```

## Secondary Operating Targets
Planning targets, not promises:

```text
Qualified prospects researched: 250+
Prospects contacted:           150+
Live sales conversations:       25+
Demos:                           5+
Pilots discussed:                2+
Paying clients:                  1+
```

## Product Positioning
Do not sell “an AI receptionist”. Commodity receptionist tools exist cheaply.

Sell a done-for-you lead-recovery system including:

- configuration
- business rules
- qualification logic
- routing
- follow-up
- logging/reporting
- optimisation
- support

The value proposition is recovered revenue and reduced lead leakage.

## Project Rule
Revenue is the project. Software is only built to support acquiring and serving the first customer.
