# UK Plumbing Market & Software Reference

## Operating Reality
Small UK plumbing/heating companies commonly operate with engineers away from a desk for most of the day. Core workflow:

```text
Inbound enquiry/call
→ job created/scheduled
→ engineer travels/attends
→ notes/photos/forms/materials
→ quote/invoice
→ payment/reconciliation
```

The commercial wedge is before job creation: an unanswered inbound lead may disappear before it ever reaches the job-management system.

### Field / Job Management
Common products worth recognising during discovery:

- ServiceM8
- Tradify
- Powered Now
- Jobber

### Accounting

### Lead Sources
Common signals:

- Google Business Profile / Google Search
- Checkatrade

### Communications

- SMS
- email
- WhatsApp

## Integration Strategy

### Client already uses a job-management platform
Good. V1 sends a structured lead notification and leaves their existing workflow intact.

### Client asks for integration
Only then investigate whether their platform has an appropriate API/webhook path and whether the integration materially affects closing or retention.

### Client has no CRM/job-management platform
Do not turn V1 into a CRM project. Store the lead in our database and notify the owner.

## Commercial Insight
Low-cost standalone AI receptionist products exist. Therefore differentiation must be:

- done-for-you configuration
- missed-lead recovery
- qualification tailored to plumbing/heating
- escalation rules
- reporting
- optimisation
- support
- integration where justified

Never position the product as simply “AI answers your phone”.

## Tool Cost Discipline
Until a client requires otherwise:

- use existing development subscriptions
- use a minimal telephony/SMS provider
- use PostgreSQL already available to the project
- avoid extra CRM/automation subscriptions
- avoid buying lead databases before manual prospecting proves the sales motion

## Research Refresh Rule
Market/software/pricing/legal facts can change. Before relying on a current price, API capability, licence term or outreach rule, re-check the primary source rather than treating this file as permanent truth.
